# Vocabulary Review — Part A

- **apparently** — it seems to be true.
- **artistic** — good at art, drawing, or music.
- **awareness** — knowing that something exists.
- **be about to (do something)** — ready to do something very soon.
- **be expecting (a baby)** — pregnant.
- **be in charge of** — to be the leader or responsible for something.
- **be situated on** — located in a specific place.
- **blame** *(n)* — saying someone caused a problem.
- **brake** *(n, v)* — *(n)* part that stops a vehicle; *(v)* to stop a vehicle.
- **chase** *(n, v)* — *(v)* to run after someone; *(n)* running after someone.
- **coach** *(n, v)* — *(n)* a sports trainer; *(v)* to train someone.
- **competitor** — someone trying to win against you.
- **cure** *(n, v)* — *(n)* medicine that heals; *(v)* to make someone healthy.
- **cycle** *(n, v)* — *(n)* a repeating process, or a bike; *(v)* to ride a bike.
- **defeat** *(n, v)* — *(v)* to beat someone; *(n)* losing a game or fight.
- **divide** *(v)* — to separate into parts.
- **drop** *(n, v)* — *(v)* to let fall; *(n)* a tiny bit of liquid.

---

- **emotional** — having or causing strong feelings.
- **emphasize** — to show something is important.
- **export** *(n, v)* — *(v)* to sell goods abroad; *(n)* goods sold abroad.
- **flood** *(n, v)* — *(n)* too much water on dry land; *(v)* to cover with water.
- **former** — from the past; earlier.
- **free** *(adj)* — costing nothing, or not busy.
- **frequent** — happening often.
- **get in** — to arrive, or enter a car/building.
- **get in touch** — to contact someone.
- **get rid of** — to throw away or remove.
- **get the phone** — to answer a ringing phone.
- **get worse** — to become more bad or sick.
- **get (something) wrong** — to make a mistake.
- **guidance** — help and advice.
- **hang out** *(v)* — to spend free time with friends.
- **interact** — to talk or work with others.
- **invest** — to put money or time for future results.

---

- **investigate** — to look for facts and truth.
- **involve** — to include as a necessary part.
- **involvement** — taking part in something.
- **iron** *(n, v)* — *(n)* a metal, or a clothes iron; *(v)* to press clothes.
- **judgment** — ability to decide well, or an opinion.
- **keep** — to continue having or staying.
- **little...** — small *(**little by little** = slowly)*.
- **loan** *(n, v)* — *(n)* borrowed money; *(v)* to lend money or things.
- **mood** — how you feel right now.
- **not only** — more than just one thing.
- **participate** — to join in an activity.
- **preference** — liking one thing more.
- **protest** *(n, v)* — *(v)* to disagree publicly; *(n)* a public complaint.
- **psychological** — related to the mind and feelings.
- **psychology** — study of the mind and behavior.
- **pump** *(n, v)* — *(n)* machine that moves liquid/air; *(v)* to move liquid/air.
- **purpose** — the reason for doing something.
- **quarrel** *(n, v)* — *(n)* an angry argument; *(v)* to argue angrily.

---

- **regardless** — anyway; without being stopped by something.
- **related** *(adj)* — connected, or in the same family.
- **remark** *(n, v)* — *(n)* a short comment; *(v)* to comment.
- **rescue** *(n, v)* — *(v)* to save from danger; *(n)* saving someone.
- **research** *(n)* — careful study to find new facts.
- **researcher** — someone who studies to find new facts.
- **result** — what happens because of something else.
- **roughly** — approximately; about.
- **satisfied** *(adj)* — happy with what you got.
- **skillful** — very good at doing something.
- **social** — liking people, or connected to society.
- **start off** — to begin.
- **theory** — an idea explaining how something works.
- **understand** — to know the meaning.
- **universal** — true everywhere for everyone.
- **unlike** — different from.
- **vary** — to change or be different.
- **vision** — eyesight, or an idea of the future.
